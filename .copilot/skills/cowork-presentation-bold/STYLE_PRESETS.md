# Style Presets

Use these presets to avoid generic presentation visuals.

## 1) Editorial Contrast

Intent:
- Executive and strategy narratives

Visuals:
- Light background with structured dark typography
- Accent color only for decisions and callouts
- Thin rule lines and generous whitespace

Fonts:
- Heading: Fraunces or Cormorant Garamond
- Body: Manrope or Public Sans

## 2) Signal and Steel

Intent:
- Product launch, technical comparison, high-stakes decisions

Visuals:
- Dark graphite base, warm amber accent
- Subtle grid texture and edge highlights
- Strong hierarchy in headings

Fonts:
- Heading: Sora or Clash Display
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans or IBM Plex Sans

## 3) Soft Future

Intent:
- Change management, adoption plans, people-first stories

Visuals:
- Light gradient layers with calm contrast
- Rounded panels with minimal borders
- Friendly but precise data presentation

Fonts:
- Heading: DM Serif Display or Playfair Display
- Body: Nunito Sans or Source Sans 3

## Animation Baseline

Use one sequence per deck:
1. Heading fade-up
2. Supporting content staggered by 80-120ms
3. No repeating bounce or overshoot effects

## Prohibited Patterns

1. Purple-on-white default gradient themes
2. Repeating the same 3-card template on every slide
3. Tiny fonts below readable clamp minimum
4. Decorative animation that obscures content
